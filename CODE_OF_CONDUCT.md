# Code of Conduct

We are committed to providing a welcoming, inclusive, and harassment-free community.

## Our Standards
- Be respectful, constructive, and friendly.
- Encourage collaborative discussion.
- Focus on what is best for the community.
- Accept constructive criticism gracefully.

## Enforcement
Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior can be reported to the project maintainers. The project team will review and investigate all complaints.
